Session 9: Advanced Document Skills

Why Master Word? 💼

Microsoft Word is the most important document tool for school and work. Mastering it significantly improves your grades and professional prospects!

Word Skills Needed For:

  • Research papers (high school & college)
  • Professional resumes and cover letters
  • Business reports and presentations
  • Academic submissions
  • Career advancement

Creating Research Papers 📚

Professional research papers have a specific structure that teachers expect.

Standard Research Paper Sections:

  1. Title Page: Title, author, date, school (formatted specially)
  2. Table of Contents: Auto-generated from headings
  3. Introduction: Overview of topic
  4. Body Sections: Main content with headings
  5. Conclusion: Summary of findings
  6. Works Cited/Bibliography: List of sources

Styles & Formatting 🎨

Use Styles to format your document professionally.

Important Styles to Use:

  • Title Style: For your paper's main title
  • Heading 1: Main sections (Introduction, Methods, etc.)
  • Heading 2: Subsections within sections
  • Normal/Body Text: Regular paragraphs
  • Quote: For block quotes

Why: Using styles makes formatting consistent and automatic

Page Numbers & Headers 📄

Professional papers include page numbers and headers.

Adding Page Numbers:

  1. Go to Insert tab
  2. Click "Page Number" → choose position (top right)
  3. Word automatically numbers every page!

Header Format (for research papers):

  • Last name in top right corner
  • Format: "Smith 1", "Smith 2", etc.

Table of Contents 📑

A Table of Contents shows readers what topics are covered and where.

How to Create TOC:

  1. Use Heading 1 and Heading 2 styles for all sections
  2. Go to References tab
  3. Click "Table of Contents"
  4. Word pulls in all headings automatically!
  5. Update it if you change section titles

Citations & Bibliography 📖

Properly cite sources to avoid plagiarism!

Citation Styles:

  • MLA: Most common for humanities & high school
  • APA: Used in science and psychology
  • Chicago: Used for history papers

How to Add Citations:

  1. Go to References tab
  2. Click "Insert Citation"
  3. Enter source info (author, title, publication)
  4. Word formats it correctly automatically!
  5. Word generates bibliography from your citations

Inserting & Captioning Figures 🖼️

Research papers often include images, charts, and diagrams.

Adding Figures Professionally:

  1. Insert image (Insert → Pictures)
  2. Right-click image → Insert Caption
  3. Word auto-numbers: "Figure 1: [description]"
  4. Creates automatic List of Figures
  5. Add alt-text for accessibility

Track Changes for Editing ✏️

When collaborating or getting feedback, use Track Changes to see exactly what was modified.

How It Works:

  1. Go to Review tab
  2. Click "Track Changes"
  3. All edits show as colored marks (red for deletions, blue for additions)
  4. You can accept/reject changes
  5. Great for getting teacher feedback!

Formatting Best Practices ✅

Professional Document Standards:

  • Font: Times New Roman 12pt (standard for academic writing)
  • Spacing: Double-spaced (except title, which is single)
  • Margins: 1 inch on all sides (default)
  • Paragraph indent: 0.5 inch for first line
  • No extra spaces: One space after periods
  • Alignment: Left-aligned body text

Export & Sharing 💾

Save and share documents properly.

File Formats:

  • .docx: Standard Word format (editable)
  • .pdf: Read-only, preserved formatting (submit to teachers)
  • .txt: Plain text (avoid for formatted documents)

Exporting to PDF:

  1. File → Export As → PDF
  2. PDF preserves all formatting
  3. Teachers can't accidentally mess up your formatting

Common Mistakes ❌

Avoid These:

  • Not using styles (manually formatting everything)
  • Inconsistent font/size (looks unprofessional)
  • Forgetting citations (plagiarism!)
  • Not double-spacing (teachers expect it)
  • Incorrect margins (many rubrics check this)
  • Missing page numbers (required in research papers)
  • Not proofreading (spelling/grammar matter!)

What We Learned 🎓

  • Styles keep formatting consistent throughout
  • Table of Contents auto-generates from headings
  • Page numbers and headers follow academic standards
  • Citations (MLA, APA, Chicago) properly credit sources
  • Figures & captions add visual information professionally
  • Track Changes helps with collaboration and feedback
  • Professional documents follow specific formatting rules!
