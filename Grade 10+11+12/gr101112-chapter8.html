Chapter 8: Designing an AI Solution

Turning Real Problems into AI-Powered Systems

Welcome to Chapter 8!

Now that you understand how AI works, it's time to design your own AI solution. In this chapter, you'll identify real-world problems, determine what data is needed, and select the right type of AI model to solve your chosen challenge. This is where your final project truly begins.

What You'll Learn:

  • How to identify and scope real-world problems
  • How to analyze feasibility and user needs
  • What types of data AI systems require
  • Privacy and ethical considerations in data collection
  • How to choose the right AI model for a specific problem
  • Trade-offs between different AI approaches

The Three Sessions in This Chapter:
