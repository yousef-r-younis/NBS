Chapter 7: Foundations of Large Language Models

Understanding How Modern AI Systems Actually Work

Welcome to Chapter 7!

Before building AI solutions, you need to understand how modern AI systems like ChatGPT actually work. This chapter has three lessons: How LLMs Work (Part 1) — tokens, training data, and what LLMs can do; How LLMs Work (Part 2) — context windows, hallucinations, and when not to use AI; and AI in the Real World — an analysis framework and case studies (ChatGPT, Grammarly, Spotify) so you can see what makes AI succeed or fail.

What You'll Learn:

  • Lesson 1: Tokens, training data, neural networks (high level), and what LLMs can do
  • Lesson 2: Context windows, hallucinations, limitations vs capabilities, and when NOT to use AI
  • Lesson 3: AI product analysis framework, case studies (OpenAI/ChatGPT, Grammarly, Spotify), problem–solution fit, and what makes AI succeed or fail

The Three Lessons in This Chapter:

💡 Big Idea:

Large Language Models don’t “think” — they predict the next token based on patterns learned from data. Understanding this, plus how real products use AI (and when they shouldn’t), changes how you use and build AI forever.
