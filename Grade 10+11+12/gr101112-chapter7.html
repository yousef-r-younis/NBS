Chapter 7: Foundations of Large Language Models

Understanding How Modern AI Systems Actually Work

Welcome to Chapter 7!

Before building AI solutions, you need to understand how modern AI systems like ChatGPT actually work. In this chapter, you'll explore tokens, training data, context windows, hallucinations, and the real capabilities and limitations of Large Language Models (LLMs). This is the foundation for everything that follows.

What You'll Learn:

  • What tokens are and how text is processed by LLMs
  • How large language models are trained
  • What a context window is and why it matters
  • Why AI systems hallucinate
  • The real strengths and limitations of LLMs
  • When to use — and when NOT to use — AI tools

💡 Big Idea:

Large Language Models don’t “think” — they predict the next token based on patterns learned from data. Understanding this changes how you use AI forever.
