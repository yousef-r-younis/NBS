Teaching AI to Understand Language

Session 2: Natural Language Processing (NLP)

What is NLP?

Making computers understand and generate human language — powering translators, chatbots, and assistants.

Examples You Use

  • Siri, Google Translate, ChatGPT, autocorrect
  • Search engines and summarizers

Why Language Is Hard

  • Sarcasm and tone
  • Ambiguity ("I saw her duck")
  • Cultural and slang differences

How LLMs Work - Simplified

Large Language Models are trained on massive text corpora to predict the next word (tokens) using billions of parameters.

Tokens Explained

Text is broken into tokens (words or subword pieces) that the model processes.

Predicting the Next Word

LLMs learn to predict likely continuations, which enables generation, summarization, and translation.

Transformers & Attention

The transformer architecture uses attention to weight which words are most relevant to each other — allowing context-aware outputs.

Context Windows

Models have limits on how much text they can "remember" at once — the context window size affects performance on long documents.

Applications

  • Chatbots and virtual assistants
  • Translation
  • Content generation and summarization

Limitations

  • No true understanding — pattern matching, not human comprehension
  • Hallucinations and factual errors
  • Bias in outputs from biased training data

Activity: Ambiguous Sentences (6 mins)

Small groups: Give examples of ambiguous sentences and discuss why they're hard for AI to interpret.

Exam Points

  • Definition of NLP and LLMs
  • Tokens, transformers, attention
  • Applications and limitations

Teaching AI to Understand Language

Session 2: NLP Basics

What is NLP?

Making computers understand and generate human language — used in assistants, translation, summarization.

Why Language Is Hard

  • Sarcasm and tone
  • Ambiguity and multiple meanings
  • Cultural and slang differences

Ambiguity Example

"I saw her duck" — could mean a bird or the action of ducking. Context decides the meaning.

How LLMs Work (Simplified)

  • Trained on massive text datasets
  • Break text into tokens
  • Predict the next token given context

Tokens & Parameters

Tokens are word pieces; large models have many parameters that store learned patterns (billions).

Context Windows

How much text a model can 'remember' at once — limits how much it can reason about in a single prompt.

Transformers & Attention

Transformers use attention to learn which words matter to each other, enabling powerful language understanding and generation.

Attention Example

"The animal didn't cross the street because IT was too tired" — attention helps link IT to the animal, not the street.

Applications

  • Chatbots and virtual assistants
  • Translation
  • Content generation and summarization

Limitations

  • No true understanding — pattern matching
  • Hallucinations and factual errors
  • Bias in outputs

Small Group Activity (6 mins)

Give examples of ambiguous sentences and discuss why they're difficult for AI.

Key Concepts for Exam

  • What tokens and context windows are
  • Transformer and attention basics
