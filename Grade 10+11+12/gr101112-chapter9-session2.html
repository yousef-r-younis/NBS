AI and Climate Change

Session 2: Carbon Footprint vs. Climate Solutions

AI's Energy Use

Large models and data centers consume significant electricity, often powered by fossil fuels.

Training vs. Inference

Training huge models is energy-intensive; inference at scale (serving millions) also adds up.

Carbon Footprint Examples

  • Estimating CO2 for model training runs
  • Data center cooling and hardware lifecycle impacts

Efficiency Improvements

Techniques: model distillation, pruning, efficient architectures, and hardware accelerators (TPUs)

Renewable-Powered Data Centers

Some providers source renewables; location and grid mix matter for true emissions reductions.

AI for Climate Solutions

  • Optimizing energy grids
  • Improved climate modeling and prediction
  • Smart agriculture and resource efficiency

Trade-offs

Weigh the emissions cost of AI tools against the emissions savings they can enable (optimization, forecasting).

Policy & Corporate Action

Reporting emissions, setting efficiency targets, and investing in renewables are key steps.

Class Activity (6 mins)

Group: List one AI application and discuss whether its climate benefit outweighs its cost.

Exam Points

  • Understand training vs. inference emissions
  • Know efficiency methods (distillation, pruning)
  • Examples of AI climate solutions
