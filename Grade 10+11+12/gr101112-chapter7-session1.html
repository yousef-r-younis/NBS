Digital Footprint 👣

What the Internet Knows About You

Session 4: Online Presence & Privacy

What Is a Digital Footprint? 🔍

Everything you post online, everything you search for, every site you visit, every purchase you make — all of it creates a permanent trail on the internet.

Reality:

  • Colleges search your name online (some have admission teams dedicated to this)
  • Employers Google you before interviews
  • Banks check your online behavior when evaluating credit
  • Everything you post can be copied, shared, and never disappears
  • AI companies scrape social media to train their models

Google Yourself ⚠️

Step 1 (Do this right now!):

  1. Go to google.com
  2. Search your full name in quotes: "Your Name"
  3. Add your city if common name: "Your Name" Portland
  4. Look at the results

Reality check: What do you see? Is it what a college/employer would see?

What's On Your Social Media? 📱

Audit Your Accounts:

  • Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X, Snapchat, Facebook - Do you have accounts?
  • Public vs. Private: Are these accounts public (anyone can see) or private?
  • Content: Is there anything you'd be embarrassed about if a parent/teacher/employer saw?
  • Location data: Are you broadcasting where you are?
  • Tags: Are other people tagging you in things?

Hard Truth:

Even private posts can be screenshotted and shared. Nothing is truly private online.

Social Media Privacy Settings: Your Shield 🛡️

On Every Platform:

  • Make accounts PRIVATE: Only approved followers see your content
  • Disable location tracking: Don't broadcast where you are
  • Limit who can tag you: Approve tags before they appear
  • Review past posts: Delete or unpost anything compromising
  • Privacy controls: Check platform settings monthly (they change!)

LinkedIn: Your Professional Profile 💼

What is LinkedIn?

Professional networking site. Employers and recruiters use it. Colleges might look at it too.

Create Your LinkedIn Now:

  1. Go to linkedin.com
  2. Create a professional profile with recent headshot
  3. Write a brief bio (50-100 words about you)
  4. List your education and any experience
  5. Connect with teachers, mentors, industry professionals

Goal: Your LinkedIn should show your best self

Professional Email Address 📧

Email Your Employers Will See:

  • BAD: partygirl2008@, dankmaximum@, sillyface91@
  • GOOD: firstname.lastname@gmail.com, or similar

Why?

Emails are professional. First-name emails show maturity and professionalism. Companies judge you before they even open your resume.

Action:

If your email is unprofessional, create a new one today for applications.

Cleaning Up Your Digital Footprint 🧹

Step 1: Social Media Audit

  • Go through all your posts from the past year
  • Delete anything inappropriate/embarrassing
  • Unpost things that don't represent your best self

Step 2: Privacy Lock-Down

  • Make all accounts private
  • Turn off location tracking
  • Approve tags before they post
  • Use strong, unique passwords

Step 3: Going Forward

  • Before you post: "Would I want a college/employer to see this?"
  • Think 5 years into the future: "Will this haunt me?"

Search Your Friends' Tags 🔎

Uncomfortable Truth:

You can control your posts, but not what others post about you.

What to do:

  • Ask friends not to tag you in embarrassing photos
  • Review tags regularly on photos of you
  • Request removal if necessary
  • Monitor what's searchable: Google "Your Name" photos monthly

This matters: One viral TikTok or group photo could affect your future

Data & AI Scraping 🤖

Reality:

AI companies (OpenAI, Google, Meta) scrape the internet to train their AI models. Your posts are being used to train chatbots.

What this means:

  • Information from your social media is in AI training data
  • Your words might appear in AI outputs
  • Your images might have been seen by AI vision models

What you can do:

  • Post less personal information
  • Make accounts private
  • Understand: once online, assume it's been scraped

Student Activity: Digital Audit 🔍

Complete This Assignment:

  1. Google yourself: What shows up? (screenshot for portfolio)
  2. Audit all accounts: List what you find about yourself
  3. Delete 5 things: Remove posts that don't represent you
  4. Privacy check: Make sure accounts are private
  5. Create action plan: How will you maintain a better digital footprint?
  6. LinkedIn: Create your professional profile

Deliverable: Written summary of your digital footprint audit & action plan

The Golden Rule 👑

Before you post, ask yourself:

  • "Would I want my parents to see this?"
  • "Would I want a college to see this?"
  • "Could this be misunderstood?"
  • "Will I regret this in 5 years?"
  • "Am I okay with this being permanent?"

If you answer 'no' to any of these: Don't post it.

What We Learned 🎓

  • Digital footprint: Everything online creates permanent trail
  • Colleges & employers search you before admitting/hiring
  • Privacy matters: Make accounts private, audit past content
  • LinkedIn = professional profile for career opportunities
  • Professional email is critical for applications
  • You can't control others' posts about you
  • AI companies scrape data from social media
  • Think before posting: Is this the image I want to project?
