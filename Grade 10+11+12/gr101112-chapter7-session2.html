Context Windows & Memory

LLMs have limited memory

Think: Short-term memory for AI 🧠

How It Works

  • LLMs don't remember past conversations (unless the app saves them)
  • Each conversation has a "context window"
  • Once you exceed it, the AI "forgets" the beginning

Example: 10,000-word conversation with an 8,000-word limit
→ Forgets the first 2,000 words

Hallucinations

When AI makes things up

⚠️ Important

The AI isn't "lying" — it doesn't know it's wrong!

What Are Hallucinations?

When an LLM confidently states false information

  • It's not lying — it doesn't "know" it's wrong
  • It's predicting plausible-sounding text, not accessing facts
  • Can be very convincing and realistic!

Why Do Hallucinations Happen?

3 Main Reasons

1. Pattern matching without understanding

2. Training data gaps

3. Ambiguous prompts

Reason 1: Pattern Matching

1 Pattern matching without understanding

LLM sees: "The capital of France is Paris"

LLM learns pattern: "[Country] capital is [City]"

Problem: If asked about a made-up country, it might invent a plausible capital!

It's following patterns, not understanding reality

Reason 2: Training Data Gaps

2 Gaps in training data

If the LLM wasn't trained on something, it guesses based on similar things

Example 1:

Asking about a very recent event (after training cutoff)

Example 2:

Asking about a tiny village with no online presence

The AI fills in blanks with "reasonable" guesses

Reason 3: Ambiguous Prompts

3 Unclear questions

Unclear questions → AI fills in gaps with assumptions

Bad Prompt Example:

"Tell me about the Paris shooting"

Which one? When? Where? Which Paris (France? Texas?)?

The AI might make assumptions and present them as facts!

Real-World Examples

1. Fake Legal Cases

Lawyers used ChatGPT to write a legal brief — it cited completely made-up court cases!

2. Fake Citations

Students ask for sources → LLM invents realistic-looking but non-existent articles

3. Biographical Errors

LLM combines facts from multiple people with similar names

⚠️ These are serious problems in real usage!

Other Important Limitations

Beyond hallucinations and memory...

Limitation 1: No Real-Time Information

📅

Knowledge Cutoff Date

LLMs don't browse the internet (unless given that tool)

  • Training data has a cutoff date
  • ChatGPT (early 2025): cutoff around October 2023
  • Can't tell you today's weather, stock prices, or breaking news

Without search tools, LLMs are stuck in the past!

Limitation 2: No True Understanding

🤖

Pattern Recognition ≠ Understanding

  • LLMs recognize patterns but don't truly "understand"
  • No lived experience, emotions, or common sense
  • Can't grasp ethical implications the way humans can
Example:

"Can you put a cat in a washing machine?"

LLM might explain HOW (physically possible) but not fully grasp the ethical/animal welfare issue

Limitation 3: Biased Training Data

⚖️

Biased Data → Biased AI

If trained on biased internet data → biased outputs

Job Recommendations:

Might suggest "nurse" for women, "engineer" for men

Language Quality:

Performs better in English than low-resource languages

Cultural Assumptions:

May reflect Western perspectives more than others

We'll dive much deeper into bias in Week 9!

Key Takeaways 🎯

1. LLMs have limited memory (context windows)

2. They hallucinate — make up plausible-sounding false info

3. They don't have real-time data (knowledge cutoff)

4. They don't truly "understand" — just predict patterns

5. They reflect biases from training data
