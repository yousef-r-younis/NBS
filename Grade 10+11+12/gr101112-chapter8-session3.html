Future of AI 🚀

What's Coming Next (2025-2035)

Session 9: Emerging Technologies & Your Future

The Pace is Accelerating ⚡

AI development is exponential. What seemed impossible 5 years ago is routine today. What seems impossible now will be standard in 5 years.

Reality:

  • ChatGPT: Went from 0 to 100M users in 2 months (2022)
  • GPT-4: Better than 90% of humans on bar exam
  • AI generated 1 billion images on DALL-E (1 year)
  • Next version will be 10x better
  • We're in the early days of AI. Like internet in 1995.

Multimodal AI: The Next Wave 🎯

What is it?

AI that understands text, images, audio, video, and combines them. Closer to how humans think.

Examples (Already Happening):

  • GPT-4V (Vision): Upload an image, ask it questions about it
  • Claude 3 Sonnet: Analyzes images of documents, charts, photos
  • OpenAI Omni: Real-time voice interaction (speech-to-speech)

The Impact:

  • AI understands context better (not just text)
  • Becomes more human-like in interaction
  • New job applications (video analysis, design feedback)

AI Agents: Autonomous AI 🤖

What?

AI that doesn't need a prompt for every task. It sets goals, makes plans, executes, and reports.

Example:

"Research this company for me." AI then:

  1. Opens browser
  2. Searches multiple sites
  3. Summarizes findings
  4. Creates presentation
  5. Sends you the result

Timeline:

  • 2024-2025: Basic agents (limited tasks)
  • 2025-2026: General agents (many tasks)
  • 2027+: Autonomous workers replacing jobs

AR/VR & AI Integration 🥽

Emerging Soon:

  • AR glasses + AI: Real-time translation, information overlay, AI assistant in your view
  • VR training: AI-powered simulations for medical/engineering/military training
  • Metaverse: Virtual worlds with AI NPCs (non-player characters)
  • Real-time collaboration: AI helping teams work in virtual spaces

Jobs Coming:

  • VR experience designers
  • AI avatar creators
  • Virtual world architects

Quantum Computing: Game Changer 💻

What is it?

Quantum computers solve certain problems exponentially faster than regular computers.

Timeline:

  • 2025-2027: First practical quantum computers (solving real problems)
  • 2028-2030: Break current encryption (will need new security)
  • 2030+: Transform drug discovery, materials science, AI

The Impact:

  • AI 100x faster
  • Problems solved that were impossible (protein folding, climate modeling)
  • Cybersecurity needs overhaul
  • Huge opportunities for physicists and engineers

Biotechnology & AI 🧬

The Combination: Gene Editing + AI

  • Disease prevention: AI designs custom vaccines in weeks (not years)
  • Gene therapy: AI identifies genetic fixes for diseases
  • Cancer treatment: AI designs personalized cancer therapy
  • Aging: Research into reversing aging (AI accelerates discovery)

Jobs & Careers:

  • Biomedical engineers
  • Computational biologists
  • Genetic counselors
  • Drug discovery scientists

Climate & Energy: AI Solutions 🌍

AI helping solve climate change:

  • Energy optimization: AI optimizes solar/wind placement and power distribution
  • Carbon capture: AI designs better materials for capturing CO2
  • Modeling: AI simulates climate scenarios to test solutions
  • Agriculture: AI reduces water/pesticide use (precision farming)

Opportunities:

  • Climate scientists with AI skills are in high demand
  • Clean tech companies hiring aggressively
  • New fields: carbon engineering, energy systems design

Skill Development for Your Future 🎓

To Thrive in AI Future:

  • Technical: Python/coding, data science, AI literacy (you have this now!)
  • Domain expertise: Deep knowledge in your field (AI + domain = most valuable)
  • Human skills: Creativity, critical thinking, emotional intelligence, leadership (AI can't replicate)
  • Adaptability: Learn constantly, embrace change
  • Ethics: Understand responsible AI (this matters more every year)

Action:

Pick one area that fascinates you. Go deep. Combine with AI knowledge.

Student Activity: Future Prediction 🔮

Write Your Vision:

  1. Pick ONE way AI will change the world in 10 years (healthcare, environment, education, etc.)
  2. Describe it in detail: How will it work? What's the impact? Who benefits? Who is harmed?
  3. What skills would you need to work in this field?
  4. What ethical issues would emerge?
  5. How would society adapt?

Deliverable: 2-3 page essay with vision, skills needed, ethical considerations

The Optimistic View 💚

If We Get This Right:

  • Medicine: Diseases cured before they spread
  • Climate: Carbon removed faster than we emit
  • Poverty: AI helps lift people out of poverty
  • Education: Everyone gets personalized tutoring
  • Work: Humans do creative/strategic work, AI does routine work
  • Discovery: New medicines, materials, clean energy invented

This is possible. We just need to be intentional about it.

The Challenging View ⚠️

Risks if We Don't Plan:

  • Inequality: AI benefits wealthy, leaves others behind
  • Jobs: Massive job displacement without retraining
  • Privacy: Total surveillance becomes possible
  • Misinformation: Can't trust any media (deepfakes)
  • AI alignment: AI systems pursue goals in harmful ways

This is also possible. Which future do you want?

Your Role in the Future 🌟

You Are the Future Builders:

  • You'll vote on AI regulation
  • You'll work in AI-powered careers
  • You'll decide what AI research to fund
  • You'll teach the next generation about AI
  • You'll face (and solve) AI ethics problems

Right Now:

  • Understand AI deeply (you do)
  • Think about ethics (you do)
  • Stay curious
  • Don't be passive - shape the future

What We Learned 🎓

  • Multimodal AI: Understands text + images + audio
  • AI agents: Will become autonomous workers
  • AR/VR: AI integration creating new fields
  • Quantum computing: Will solve impossible problems
  • Biotech + AI: Solving disease, aging, health
