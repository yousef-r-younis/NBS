AI's Impact on Work and Money

Session 3: Jobs & Economy

Will AI Take All Our Jobs?

Big question: history shows automation changes jobs — some disappear, others are created or augmented.

Jobs at Risk

  • Routine, repetitive, rule-based tasks
  • Examples: data entry, telemarketing, basic bookkeeping
  • Transportation may be affected by self-driving vehicles

Transportation Impact

Self-driving trucks could affect millions of drivers — think about regional and social consequences.

Customer Service & Retail

Chatbots and automated checkouts reduce some roles but create new technical and oversight jobs.

Jobs Being Created

  • AI trainers and labelers
  • Prompt engineers and data scientists
  • AI ethicists, safety engineers

Enhanced Roles

Professionals (doctors, lawyers) using AI to be more productive and make higher-level decisions.

Economic Impacts

  • Productivity gains
  • Potential widening income inequality
  • Large GDP growth projected from AI adoption

Different Countries' Approaches

  • China: heavy state-led investment and surveillance use
  • EU: regulation and worker protections
  • US: private-sector-led innovation and lighter regulation

Preparing for the Future

Focus on creativity, emotional intelligence, critical thinking, and lifelong learning to stay relevant.

Education & Retraining

Policies for retraining and lifelong learning help displaced workers transition to new roles.

THINK-PAIR-SHARE (7 mins)

Question: "What job do you want? How might AI affect it?" Discuss and share with partner.

Exam Topics

  • Jobs at risk vs. jobs created
  • Economic trade-offs and policy responses
  • Skills to develop for the AI era
