Future of AI & Emerging Technologies

Session 3: AGI, Quantum, BCIs & Societal Change

What Is AGI?

Artificial General Intelligence: systems with broad, human-level cognitive abilities—still hypothetical and debated.

Timelines & Uncertainty

Experts disagree on when (or if) AGI will arrive; planning for impacts is still important.

Safety and Alignment

Ensuring powerful systems act as intended: value alignment, interpretability, and robust testing.

Quantum Computing & AI

Quantum hardware could accelerate some AI workloads, but practical benefits are still emerging.

Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs)

BCIs may enable new interfaces and raise privacy, consent, and identity concerns.

Economic & Social Impacts

  • Acceleration of automation
  • Shifts in skills and education
  • Potential for concentration of power

Governance Challenges

Global coordination, tech monopolies, and unequal access are central governance problems.

Ethical Questions

  • What rights, if any, for advanced AI?
  • How to preserve human dignity and agency?

Class Debate (8 mins)

Proposition: "AGI will arrive within 30 years." Prepare arguments for and against.

Exam Points

  • Definitions: AGI, BCIs, quantum AI
  • Key governance and safety concerns
  • Societal and ethical trade-offs
