The Golden Rules

Maximum 6 bullet points per slide, 6 words per bullet

This keeps slides clean and forces you to be concise!

  • Visual > Text: Use images, diagrams, and icons instead of walls of text
  • Consistency: Use the same fonts, colors, and style throughout
  • Contrast: Dark text on light background (or vice versa) for readability

Tool #1: Gamma

AI-Powered Presentation Generator

What It Does

Gamma creates entire presentations from a topic or outline in minutes. You just provide a topic, and AI generates slides with text, layouts, and design.

How to Use Gamma

  • Go to gamma.app
  • Click "Create" and select "Presentation" mode
  • Type your topic (e.g., "Introduction to Neural Networks for Kids")
  • Watch AI generate slides automatically
  • Edit and customize to make it your own

âœ… Best for: Starting from scratch or needing quick structure

Tool #2: Slidesgo

Professional Templates with AI Features

What It Does

Slidesgo provides hundreds of professional templates you can customize. Many include AI-generated content suggestions to help you fill in the blanks.

How to Use Slidesgo

  • Go to slidesgo.com
  • Search for templates by topic (e.g., "technology," "education")
  • Download a template that matches your topic
  • Edit in PowerPoint to add your own content

âœ… Best for: When you want a polished, professional look without designing from scratch

Tool #3: Quillbot

AI Writing Assistant for Better Text

What It Does

Quillbot helps rewrite and improve your text. It can summarize long paragraphs into bullet points and make complicated ideas easier to understand.

How to Use Quillbot

  • Go to quillbot.com
  • Paste your text (long paragraphs, notes, etc.)
  • Use "Summarizer" to make it slide-friendly (short bullet points)
  • Use "Paraphraser" to reword difficult concepts in simpler language
  • Copy the improved text into your slides

âœ… Best for: When your text is too long, too complicated, or needs better phrasing
