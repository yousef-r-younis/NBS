What You're Designing

Over the next 4 weeks, your team will design an AI system that solves a real-world problem and pitch it at the end-of-year exhibition. The best ideas get actually built.

The Golden Rule

Start with a problem, not a technology. Don't say "we want to build a neural network." Say "students struggle to review for exams â€” here's how AI helps."
