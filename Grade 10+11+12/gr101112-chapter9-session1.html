Who Controls AI?

Session 1: AI Regulation & Governance

Why Regulate AI?

To ensure safety, fairness, prevent misuse, and protect civil rights as AI systems scale and affect more decisions.

The Challenge

Technology moves faster than law — regulators must balance innovation and protection.

EU AI Act

  • First comprehensive AI law (passed 2024)
  • Risk-based approach: unacceptable, high, limited, minimal risk
  • Some banned uses (social scoring, certain real-time facial recognition)

US Approach

  • Executive action (2023) focused on safety testing and civil rights
  • Sector-specific regulation (FDA for medical AI, FAA for aviation)

China's Strategy

  • Algorithm regulation and disclosure requirements
  • Strong state oversight and content controls

Key Regulatory Issues

  • Transparency — should companies reveal how AI works?
  • Liability — who pays when AI harms someone?
  • Competition — preventing monopolies

Industry Self-Regulation

Tech companies often publish safety commitments and do red-team testing, but enforcement and conflicts of interest limit effectiveness.
