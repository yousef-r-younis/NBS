Final Assessment 🎓

Demonstrating Your AI Literacy

Session 10: Your Comprehensive Evaluation

Assessment Overview 📋

This assessment has TWO parts:

  • Written Test (70% of grade): Essays and short answer
  • Practical Test (30% of grade): Resume and cover letter

Total time: 2-3 hours across 2-3 days

What This Tests:

  • Deep understanding of AI concepts
  • Critical thinking and ethics
  • Professional communication
  • Real skills you need for college/career

Part 1: Written Test (70%) 📝

SECTION A: Essays (Choose 3 of 5)

Answer 3 of these 5 essay prompts (10 points each):

  1. Responsible AI Use: Explain how to use AI tools ethically. Give examples of responsible vs irresponsible use of ChatGPT in school. What's the difference between using AI to learn vs cheating?
  2. AI in Your Career: Discuss a career you're interested in. How will AI impact it? What new opportunities might emerge? What skills will you need that AI can't replace?
  3. AI Ethics Dilemma: Choose one case study (facial recognition, self-driving cars, deepfakes, or job automation). Explain the ethical tensions. What would you do if you were the decision-maker?
  4. Digital Footprint & Future: Analyze your digital footprint. How might something you post today affect you in 5 years? What's your strategy for maintaining a professional online presence?
  5. Misinformation & Media: Describe 3 techniques to identify fake news and AI-generated content. Give real examples. Why is media literacy critical in 2025?

Part 1: Written Test Continued 📝

SECTION B: Short Answer (10 questions, 2 points each = 20 points)

  1. What is prompt engineering? Give one example of a good prompt.
  2. How does two-factor authentication protect you?
  3. What are the signs of a phishing email?
  4. Explain one way AI is biased. Why does this matter?
  5. What's the difference between correlation and causation in statistics?
  6. How can you use reverse image search to verify content?
  7. What is HTTPS and why should you only enter passwords on HTTPS sites?
  8. Name 3 emerging AI technologies (multimodal AI, quantum computing, etc.) and describe one.
  9. What's your personal responsibility when sharing information online?
  10. Describe one career opportunity created by AI growth.

Part 1: Grading Rubric ✅

ESSAYS (30 points total):

  • 10 points: Comprehensive, nuanced answer addressing all aspects
  • 8 points: Good answer with minor gaps
  • 6 points: Adequate answer but some key points missing
  • 4 points: Basic answer, significant gaps
  • 0-2 points: Minimal effort or incomplete

SHORT ANSWER (20 points):

  • 2 points: Correct, clear, concise
  • 1 point: Partially correct
  • 0 points: Incorrect or blank

Part 2: Practical Test (30%) 💼

CREATE BOTH (Not optional!):

  1. RESUME (1 page):
    • Professional format (name, contact, education, experience, skills)
    • Using action verbs
    • Quantified achievements
    • Appropriate for college application or first job
  2. COVER LETTER (1 page):
    • For a specific job, internship, or college
    • 3-4 paragraphs (opening, why you, qualifications, closing)
    • Professional tone
    • Shows research about the organization

Practical Test Grading 📊

RESUME (15 points):

  • Format (3 pts): Professional layout, correct margins, one page
  • Content (7 pts): Relevant experience, action verbs, quantified results
  • Grammar/Spelling (3 pts): No errors, professional language
  • Completeness (2 pts): All sections present

COVER LETTER (15 points):

  • Format (3 pts): Professional business letter format, one page
  • Content (7 pts): Addresses prompt, shows research, compelling writing
  • Grammar/Spelling (3 pts): No errors, professional tone
  • Specificity (2 pts): Tailored to specific job/org, not generic

Test Strategy & Tips 🎯

For Essays:

  • Read questions carefully - answer what's asked
  • Use examples from class, real world, news
  • Show critical thinking - don't just repeat facts
  • Address counterarguments (shows mature thinking)
  • Proofread - grammar counts

For Short Answer:

  • Be concise - 2-3 sentences is usually enough
  • Be accurate - one wrong detail can cost points
  • Answer directly - no fluff

Practical Test Strategy 💼

For Resume:

  • Research job descriptions - mirror language
  • Quantify everything - "managed $5k budget" vs "managed budget"
  • Use strong verbs - Led, Designed, Increased, not Helped
  • Format check - Print and read to catch spacing/alignment issues
  • Get feedback - Have parent/teacher review

For Cover Letter:

  • Personalize completely - Research the company/org
  • Show personality - Professional but not robotic
  • One page maximum - Concise and impactful
  • Proofread 3 times - One typo can sink you

Academic Integrity & AI Use ⚖️

IMPORTANT RULES:

  • You CAN use AI: To brainstorm, outline, research, explain concepts
  • You CANNOT: Submit AI-generated essays/answers (that's cheating)
  • You SHOULD: Write your own analysis, arguments, insights
  • Resume/Cover Letter: You write these. AI can help edit, but it's YOUR document

What Teachers Will Check:

  • Does the essay sound like you? (Too polished = flagged)
  • Can you defend your points in discussion?
  • Do you understand what you wrote?
