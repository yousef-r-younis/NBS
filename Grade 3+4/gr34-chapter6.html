AI Making a Positive Difference

Welcome to Chapter 6!

AI isn't just for entertainment — it's helping make the world better! In this chapter, you'll discover how AI makes smart predictions with autocorrect, helps people who face challenges with accessibility tools, and breaks down language barriers. These three sessions show AI's power to help and include everyone!

What You'll Learn:

  • How autocorrect and autocomplete predict what you're typing
  • How AI helps people with different abilities (text-to-speech, smart wheelchairs, etc.)
  • How AI translates between languages to connect people worldwide
  • That AI can learn from patterns in language and text
  • That technology should be accessible to everyone!

Big Idea: AI learns from patterns in massive amounts of data. Whether it's learning from billions of typed messages, understanding accessibility needs, or learning from millions of translations, AI uses these patterns to help people. This chapter shows that AI can be a force for good!
