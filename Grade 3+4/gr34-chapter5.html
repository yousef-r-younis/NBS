Understanding AI in Your Daily Life

Welcome to Chapter 5!

In this chapter, you'll explore three amazing ways AI is already part of your life! From YouTube recommendations that know what you love, to face recognition that unlocks your phone, to smart game opponents that react to your moves — AI is everywhere! Let's dive into these three sessions to understand how AI learns about you and makes your tech experiences smarter.

  • How face recognition works for Face ID and fun filters
  • How AI opponents in games think and react to your moves
  • The power of patterns — how AI finds them and uses them
  • That AI is learning about YOUR unique preferences!
