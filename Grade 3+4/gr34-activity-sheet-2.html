📚 What You Learned About AI!

AI is a smart computer that helps you every day!

The 5 Big Ideas:

  • Session 1 - AI is everywhere: In your phone, YouTube, and smart speakers like Siri and Alexa
  • Session 2 - AI sees with patterns: It looks for colors, shapes, and textures to recognize things (like finding a dog in a picture!)
  • Session 3 - AI talks by learning words: It reads millions of books and messages to learn how to chat with you
  • Session 4 - AI creates art and music: You describe what you want, and AI makes it for you!
  • Session 5 - You can build AI too: You learned to make robots, train machines, and code smart pets

⭐ Remember:

  • AI learns from patterns, not like humans
  • AI doesn't have feelings
  • AI gets smarter with more examples
  • You can create with AI!
