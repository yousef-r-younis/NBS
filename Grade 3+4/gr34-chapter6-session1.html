AI Helper #1: Text-to-Speech 🗣️

The Computer Reads to You!

What is it? AI reads text OUT LOUD so you can hear it!

Who uses it? People who can't see the screen well, or anyone who wants to listen instead of read!

How it works:

  1. AI looks at the words on screen
  2. AI figures out how to say each word
  3. AI speaks the words out loud!
📢

Examples:

📱 Phone reading messages

📚 Audiobooks

🌐 Websites reading to you

Let's Try It! 🎤

We're going to use Text-to-Speech right now!

✨ Watch as the computer reads this sentence:

"Hello! I am an AI helping you learn. AI can make the world better for everyone!"

(Teacher: Use your device's text-to-speech feature to read the text above)

AI Helper #2: Speech-to-Text ⌨️

The Computer Types What You Say!
🎤

Examples:

💬 Voice typing messages

📝 Dictating homework

🗣️ Siri, Alexa, Google Assistant

What is it? You SPEAK and AI TYPES what you said!

Who uses it? People who can't type easily, or anyone who wants to talk instead of type!

How it works:

  1. AI listens to your voice
  2. AI breaks down the sounds
  3. AI matches sounds to words
  4. AI types the words!

Let's Try It! 🎙️

We're going to use Speech-to-Text right now!

✨ Try saying this sentence:

"AI is amazing and helps people every day!"

(Teacher: Open a text document and use voice typing feature. Students can try on tablets!)

AI Helper #3: Smart Wheelchairs ♿

AI Helps People Move Safely!

Some people use wheelchairs to move around. AI can make wheelchairs SUPER SMART! 🚀

What AI-powered wheelchairs can do:

  • Avoid obstacles — AI sees walls, people, furniture and moves around them
  • Find paths — AI figures out the best way to go from here to there
  • Follow voice commands — "Go to the kitchen!" and it goes!
  • Stay balanced — AI keeps the wheelchair steady and safe

It's like a self-driving car!

AI Helper #5: Smart Hearing Aids 🦻

AI Helps You Hear Better!
👂

Some people can't hear well. Modern hearing aids have AI that makes them SUPER SMART!

What AI hearing aids do:

  • Focus on voices — Blocks out noise, keeps voices clear
  • Adjust volume — Quiet places? Louder. Loud places? Just right.
  • Recognize where sounds come from — AI knows if someone is talking behind you
  • Learn your preferences — AI remembers what you like!

Imagine being at a noisy birthday party 🎉 AI helps you hear your friends talking, not just the loud music!

Real People, Real Help! 💙

👨‍🦯 Ahmed uses AI

Ahmed can't see, but his phone uses AI to describe everything around him. He can "see" his friends, read signs, and know when a car is coming!
