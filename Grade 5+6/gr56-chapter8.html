AI, Privacy, Deepfakes, and Digital Literacy

Welcome to Chapter 8!

As AI becomes more powerful and widespread, it's essential to think critically about its impact on society and your personal life. In this chapter, you'll learn about privacy and data, how to spot deepfakes and misinformation, and how AI processes and understands human language.

What You'll Learn:

  • How AI and companies collect and use your data
  • What privacy means in the digital age
  • How deepfakes are created and why they're dangerous
  • How to identify AI-generated misinformation
  • How AI processes and understands human language
