Final Assessment 🏆

Grades 5-6 AI Curriculum Review

Session 10: Test Your Knowledge

Assessment Overview 📋

This final assessment tests your understanding of Sessions 1-9. You'll complete two parts: a written test and a practical Word project.

Assessment Structure:

  • Part 1 - Written Test: 30 points (multiple choice, short answer, diagrams)
  • Part 2 - Practical Project: 20 points (Microsoft Word document)
  • Total: 50 points (grade out of 50)

Part 1: Written Test 📝

Answer the following questions based on Sessions 1-9. Use complete sentences for short answer questions.

Section A: Multiple Choice (10 points)

  1. What is the first step in how a website loads?
    • a) Server sends HTML file
    • b) Browser sends request to server
    • c) Images load
    • d) CSS applies formatting
  2. Which of these is NOT something Google's search algorithm ranks websites by?
    • a) Relevance to search query
    • b) Website authority (backlinks)
    • c) Server location
    • d) Mobile-friendly design
  3. What are training data biases?
    • a) Mistakes in the AI's math
    • b) Problems in data the AI learned from
    • c) Viruses in the AI
    • d) Too much information

Part 1 Continued: Section B 📝

Section B: Short Answer (10 points)

  1. Explain how pixels and features help AI recognize images. (2-3 sentences)
  2. Why is it hard for AI to understand sarcasm? Give an example. (2-3 sentences)
  3. List three apps on your phone and explain what data each one collects. (2 sentences per app)

Section C: Critical Thinking (10 points)

  1. You see a viral video of a celebrity saying something shocking. How would you determine if it's a real deepfake or real footage? List 5 steps you'd take. (5 sentences)
  2. A company wants to buy your data from an app you use. What questions would you ask about how your data would be used? (3-4 sentences)

Part 2: Practical Project - Word Document 📄

Create a professional 2-page document in Microsoft Word on any topic from Sessions 1-9.

Requirements (20 points):

  • Title Page: Title, your name, date (formatted differently from body)
  • Heading Structure: Use Heading 1 for main sections, Heading 2 for subsections
  • Table of Contents: Auto-generated TOC on page 2
  • Content: 2 pages of body text (10pt + image/table)
  • Headers/Footers: Page numbers in footer
  • At least one table or image with proper formatting
  • Two citations: At least 2 sources cited (MLA format)

Project Topic Ideas 💡

Choose One:

  • "How the Internet Works: A Technical Overview"
  • "Bias in AI: Problems and Solutions"
  • "Digital Privacy: Protecting Your Data"
  • "Deepfakes and How to Spot Them"
  • "Search Engines: From Crawling to Ranking"
  • "Natural Language Processing: How AI Understands Language"
  • "Computer Vision: Teaching Machines to See"
  • "AI Recommendations and Filter Bubbles"

Grading Rubric 📊

Written Test (30 points):

  • Multiple Choice: 10 points (1 pt each)
  • Short Answer: 10 points (accuracy, detail, clarity)
  • Critical Thinking: 10 points (depth of analysis)

Practical Project (20 points):

  • Formatting & Structure: 5 points (styles, TOC, headers/footers)
  • Content Quality: 8 points (2 pages, accurate information)
  • Citations: 4 points (minimum 2 sources, MLA format, properly cited)
  • Table of Contents: 3 points (auto-generated from headings)

Success Tips for the Test 🎯

Before the Test:

  • Review notes from Sessions 1-9
  • Re-read the slides about topics you find confusing
  • Make flashcards for technical terms
  • Create a study guide with key concepts

During the Test:

  • Read questions carefully before answering
  • Show your work for short answer questions
  • Use specific examples and details
  • Double-check spelling and grammar
