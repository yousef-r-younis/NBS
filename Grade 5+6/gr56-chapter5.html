The Golden Rules 🌟

Use the 6Ã—6 Rule

Max 6 bullet points, Max 6 words per point

  • Pictures > Words: Use lots of images!
  • Keep it simple: Don't cram too much on one slide
  • Same colors: Pick 2-3 colors and stick with them
  • Easy to read: Make text big and clear
